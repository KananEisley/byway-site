Byway

The calm web.

What if the web had chosen privacy and fairness over algorithms and social media? Byway is a separate web of plain, well-made pages, reached through the Byway app for iPhone.

In beta on iPhone · Coming to the App Store

Calm by design, not by blocking

Byway pages simply can't do the things that made the web tiring. There's no way to write them into a page, and the Byway app draws every page itself, in the same careful type.

  • No pop-ups
  • No autoplay
  • No moving ads
  • No cookie banners
  • No scripts
  • No tracking
  • No algorithmic feeds

Links to the regular web open in Safari, after Byway shows you where they go, with tracking codes taken out.

What you'll find

Pages, beautifully set

Every site on Byway is read the same calm way: good type, room to breathe, nothing moving.

Search

Finds Byway pages by what they say. It keeps no logs of what anyone searches.

Your own profile

A small site of your own: a photo, your name, a few words about you, a blog and photo albums. Filled in with a simple form. No code.

Town Square

Short posts from everyone's profiles, newest first. No likes, no reposts, nothing promoted. The only number is how many replies a post has.

Messages

Between you and your contacts, from your iPhone to theirs. There's no message server keeping them.

Inner Circle

Up to 25 people you choose. Posts and albums for your Inner Circle are locked so only they can open them.

Rings

Profiles linked around a shared interest, the way webrings used to work. Join one, or start your own.

Encyclopedia, Weather, Transparency

Wikipedia articles, forecasts, and US campaign money and Congress, shown as calm Byway pages.

Private, and fair

No accounts, no passwords

Who you are is a key that stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud Keychain. Face ID opens it. It's never on our servers.

Nothing follows you

No cookies, and a page can only load things from its own site, so nobody else sees what you read.

We keep almost nothing

No logs of who visits what. Besides public site names, our servers hold only copies of what people publish for everyone, for a day, and nothing private they could read.

Nobody can buy attention

Feeds are in time order, never ranked for you. No paid or featured placement anywhere, ever.

Price

Reading Byway is free.

Your own profile, posting in Town Square and the rest are free for 30 days, then $4.99 a month. Paying never makes anyone more visible. While Byway is in beta, nothing is charged.
