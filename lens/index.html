Byway Lens

A calm camera for small photos.

Byway Lens keeps small copies of your photos in your own iCloud Drive, as ordinary files, backed up apart from Apple Photos.

$2.99 · iPhone · Coming soon to the App Store

Small on purpose

Every photo is made small: tens of thousands fit in a few hundred megabytes, and they send in an instant. Want more detail? Choose a larger size, as WebP or JPEG.

Yours, as plain files

In your iCloud Drive

Photos go to the Byway Lens folder in iCloud Drive, named for when they were taken. They're right there in Finder on your Mac, no extra app needed.

Albums are folders

Make an album in the app or a folder on your Mac. They're the same thing.

The photos you already have

Choose photos from Apple Photos, or add your whole library at once, dated when each was taken. Apple Photos is never changed.

Send them in Messages

In any conversation, tap + and Byway Lens to add a photo to your message.

A quiet camera

What you see is what you get

The viewfinder is the photo's own shape. Pinch or slide to zoom.

Nothing goes off on its own

No sounds of its own, and the flash is off unless you turn it on.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Byway Lens has no code that connects to the internet, so we never see your photos. Only the picture is saved: no location, no camera details.
