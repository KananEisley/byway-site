Byway Lens: Privacy

Byway Lens collects nothing. Your photos are yours, kept in your own iCloud Drive.

What we collect

Nothing. Byway Lens has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it has no code that connects to the internet. We never see your photos or anything about you or how you use the app.

Where your photos go

Photos you take or add are saved as small files in your iCloud Drive, in the Byway Lens folder. Apple stores and syncs that folder for you, under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. If iCloud Drive is off, photos stay on your iPhone until it's on.

Only the picture itself is saved: no location and no camera details.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only to take your photos.
  • Choosing photos from Apple Photos: uses Apple's own picker, so Byway Lens sees only the photos you choose.
  • Adding all your photos: asks to see your photo library, only to make the small copies. Nothing in Apple Photos is changed or deleted.

Kept on your iPhone

Two small notes stay on your iPhone and never leave it: which photos from Apple Photos were already added (so adding all of them again skips those), and which album the camera saves into.

Sharing

A photo leaves Byway Lens only when you share it: with the Share button, or by attaching it to a message in Messages. It then goes where you send it, through Apple's apps.

Children

Byway Lens collects nothing from anyone, children included.
