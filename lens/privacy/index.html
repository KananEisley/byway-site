Byway Lens: Privacy

Byway Lens collects nothing. Your photos are yours, kept as ordinary files in your own iCloud Drive or on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Byway Lens has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it has no code that connects to the internet. We never see your photos or anything about you or how you use the app.

Where your photos go

Photos you take or add are saved as files in your iCloud Drive, in the Byway Lens folder. Apple stores and syncs that folder for you, under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. If iCloud Drive is off, photos stay on your iPhone until it's on. In Settings you can choose to keep them only on your iPhone instead.

When you export photos, Byway Lens copies them only to the folder or drive you pick.

Location and camera details are never saved. The date a photo was taken names its file and picks its year album; inside the file, it's kept only if you turn on Keep the date taken in Settings. Otherwise each file holds nothing but the picture.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only to take your photos.
  • Choosing photos from Apple Photos: uses Apple's own picker, so Byway Lens sees only the photos you choose.
  • Adding all your photos: asks to see your photo library, only to make the copies. Nothing in Apple Photos is changed or deleted.

Kept on your iPhone

A few small notes stay on your iPhone and never leave it: which photos from Apple Photos were already added (so adding all of them again skips those), which album the camera saves into, and your choices in Settings.

Sharing

A photo leaves Byway Lens only when you send it somewhere: with the Share button, by attaching it to a message in Messages, or by exporting it to a folder or drive you pick. It then goes where you send it, through Apple's own tools.

Children

Byway Lens collects nothing from anyone, children included.
