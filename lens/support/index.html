Where are my photos?

In iCloud Drive, in the Byway Lens folder: in the Files app on your iPhone, and in Finder on your Mac. Albums are the folders inside it. If iCloud Drive is off, photos wait on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Byway Lens) and move to iCloud Drive the next time Byway Lens opens with it on.

To keep them only on your iPhone instead, choose This iPhone under Where photos are kept in Settings (the gear on the photos screen); Byway Lens moves them for you. They're then not in iCloud, so copy them somewhere safe now and then with Export.

Copying photos to a flash drive or computer

On the photos screen, tap the export button (the box with an arrow) and pick a folder: on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or on a USB-C or Lightning flash drive plugged into your iPhone. Everything is copied, with albums as folders; open an album first to copy just that one, or use Select to pick photos. If you stop part way, exporting again carries on and skips what's already there.

For copies most like your originals, add your photos at Full size as JPEG, and turn on Keep the date taken so other computers sort them by date.

What size are the photos?

You choose in Settings: Small (360 × 480, about 40 KB), Medium (720 × 960), Large (1440 × 1920) or Full (every pixel the camera took), as JPEG or WebP. Add All Photos asks each time. Small photos are small on purpose: tens of thousands fit in a few hundred megabytes, and they're ready for a Byway profile. Your photos in Apple Photos are never changed.

WebP or JPEG?

JPEG, the default, opens everywhere, even on old computers and phones. WebP files are about a third smaller and open on iPhones, iPads, Macs and modern computers. Choose in Settings; photos you already have stay as they are.

Is the date kept?

Every photo is named for when it was taken. Inside the file, nothing but the picture is kept unless you turn on Keep the date taken in Settings; then the date taken is kept too, so other computers and photo apps sort by it. Location and camera details are never kept.

Adding photos from Apple Photos

On the photos screen, tap +. Choose Photos adds the ones you pick. Add All Photos adds every photo (screenshots left out unless you switch them on), oldest first; you can stop at any time, and starting again carries on where it left off. Each photo goes into an album for the year it was taken (turn off Sort into albums by year in Settings to use the album you're looking at instead).

Photos only: videos aren't included, and Live Photos come in as still photos.

Albums

Tap New Album at the top of your photos, or make a folder in the Byway Lens folder on your Mac. To choose where the camera saves, tap the album button at the top right of the camera. Touch and hold an album to rename or delete it.

Sending a photo in Messages

In a conversation, tap +, then More, then Byway Lens, and tap a photo to add it to your message. Messages can only reach photos kept in iCloud Drive.

A new iPhone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, install Byway Lens, and your photos and albums are there. Deleting the app doesn't delete your photos from iCloud Drive. Photos kept only on your iPhone are not in iCloud: export them before you change phones.

Byway Lens keeps a second copy of your photos in an open format. It's on the same Apple Account as Apple Photos, so for a backup that's truly separate, export to a flash drive or computer now and then.
