Byway Lens: Help

Where are my photos?

In iCloud Drive, in the Byway Lens folder: in the Files app on your iPhone, and in Finder on your Mac. Albums are the folders inside it. If iCloud Drive is off, photos wait on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Byway Lens) and move to iCloud Drive the next time Byway Lens opens with it on.

Why are the photos so small?

On purpose. Each photo is at most 480 pixels on its longest side and about 30 to 80 KB, saved as WebP. Tens of thousands fit in a few hundred megabytes, they send instantly, and they're plenty for looking back at, sharing, or putting on a Byway profile. Your full-size photos in Apple Photos are never changed.

Adding photos from Apple Photos

On the photos screen, tap +. Choose Photos adds the ones you pick. Add All Photos adds every photo (screenshots left out unless you switch them on), oldest first; you can stop at any time, and starting again carries on where it left off. Photos go into the album you're looking at.

Albums

Tap New Album at the top of your photos, or make a folder in the Byway Lens folder on your Mac. To choose where the camera saves, tap the album button at the top right of the camera. Touch and hold an album to rename or delete it.

Sending a photo in Messages

In a conversation, tap +, then More, then Byway Lens, and tap a photo to add it to your message.

A new iPhone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, install Byway Lens, and your photos and albums are there. Deleting the app doesn't delete your photos from iCloud Drive.
