Rory Babies

A pleasant dress-up game for small children.

Choose a baby's skin and hair, dress them in bonnets, onesies, bibs and booties, or as a knight, an astronaut or a dinosaur, choose where they are, and keep the outfits you love. Made for children about 3 to 7, with nothing to read and nothing to win.

iPhone and iPad · Coming soon to the App Store

Lots to try on

153 things to wear and hold

Bonnets, bibs, onesies, sleepsuits, booties, pacifiers, a bottle, a rattle, a teddy bear and a rubber duck. Dress up as a chef, a wizard, an astronaut, a robot, a knight, a princess or a dinosaur, and for every holiday.

Seven colours each

Every hat, shirt and shoe comes in seven soft colours.

Babies who look like everyone

Ten skin colours from ivory to deep brown, fifteen hairstyles (or no hair yet) in nine colours, and head coverings families wear: a kippah, a kufi, a patka, a headwrap and a hijab.

Twenty places

A nursery, bath time, a playground, a birthday party, a picnic, a farm, a castle, an alien planet, the moon and more.

Easy for small hands

No words to read

Everything is a picture, with big buttons. Tap a thing to put it on, and tap again to take it off.

Favourites to come back to

The heart keeps an outfit in a drawer of favourites. Start fresh any time, and put it all back with one tap.

A surprise

The sparkles make a surprise outfit.

Pleasant, and safe

  • No ads
  • No score or timer
  • No winning or losing
  • No rewards for coming back
  • Nothing collected

Rory Babies has no code that connects to the internet, and nothing a child does in it ever leaves the device. Nothing moves on its own, and there's nothing to buy by accident.
