Rory Babies: Privacy

Rory Babies collects nothing from anyone, children included. Everything stays on the device it's played on.

What we collect

Nothing. Rory Babies has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and nothing from other companies built in, and it has no code that connects to the internet. We never see anything about a child, a grown-up, or how the game is played.

Kept on the device

Rory Babies keeps a few things on the iPhone or iPad, and they never leave it:

  • The baby and their outfit, so they're still there next time.
  • Saved outfits, kept with the heart (up to 60).

Deleting the app deletes all of these.

Nothing leaves the game

Rory Babies has no camera button, no Share button and nothing in the Files app, so nothing a child makes can be sent or copied anywhere from it.

No permissions asked

Rory Babies doesn't ask to use the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Children

Rory Babies is made for young children. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.
