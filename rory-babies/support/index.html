Dressing the baby

The pictures along the bottom (beside the baby on a wide iPad) are drawers. Tap a drawer, then tap a thing to put it on, and tap it again to take it off. Each drawer scrolls sideways when it holds more than fits. The baby wears one thing in each place at a time: on their head, over their eyes, on their face, round their neck, their clothes, on their back, their shoes, and something to hold. A diaper always stays on under everything.

The row of coloured dots changes the colour of what's in that drawer.

The first drawers choose the baby: their skin, and their hair (the dots below choose the hair's colour). The place drawer, at the end, chooses where they are.

The buttons over the baby

  • Start fresh (the round arrow, top left): takes everything off, keeping the baby's skin, hair and place. Tap it again, before changing anything, to put it all back.
  • Sparkles: a surprise outfit.
  • Heart: keeps the outfit as a favourite. It fills in when the outfit is already kept.

Favourite outfits

Kept outfits are in the first drawer, the one with hearts in it. Tap one to dress the baby in it again. To remove some, tap the bin at the end of the row, tap the ones to remove, then tap the tick.

Is it safe for my child?

Rory Babies has no ads, no links out, no Share button and no purchases inside the game, and it never connects to the internet. There's no score, timer or reward for coming back, so it's easy to put down. See the privacy policy.
