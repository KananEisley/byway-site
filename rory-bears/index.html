Rory Bears

A pleasant dress-up game for small children.

Dress Rory the bear in hats, glasses, clothes, wings and more, choose where Rory stands, and take pictures. Made for children about 3 to 7, with nothing to read and nothing to win.

iPhone and iPad · Coming soon to the App Store

Lots to try on

42 things to wear and hold

Hats and crowns, glasses and goggles, scarves and ties, dresses, sweaters and pajamas, capes and wings, boots and flippers, balloons and ice cream.

Seven colours each

Every hat, shirt and shoe comes in seven soft colours.

Five bears

Honey, cocoa, cream, grey or panda.

Five places

A garden, the snow, the beach, a starry night, or at home.

Easy for small hands

No words to read

Everything is a picture, with big buttons. Tap a thing to put it on, tap again to take it off, or drag it onto Rory.

Favourites to come back to

The heart keeps an outfit in a drawer of favourites. Start fresh any time, and put it all back with one tap.

Pictures of Rory

The camera keeps a picture of Rory in an album in the game. Grown-ups can copy pictures out with the Files app.

A surprise

The sparkles dress Rory in a surprise outfit.

Pleasant, and safe

  • No ads
  • No score or timer
  • No winning or losing
  • No rewards for coming back
  • Nothing collected

Rory Bears has no code that connects to the internet, and nothing a child does in it ever leaves the device. Nothing moves on its own, and there's nothing to buy by accident.
