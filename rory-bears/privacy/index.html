Rory Bears: Privacy

Rory Bears collects nothing from anyone, children included. Everything stays on the device it's played on.

What we collect

Nothing. Rory Bears has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and nothing from other companies built in, and it has no code that connects to the internet. We never see anything about a child, a grown-up, or how the game is played.

Kept on the device

Rory Bears keeps a few things on the iPhone or iPad, and they never leave it:

  • Rory's outfit, so it's still there next time.
  • Saved outfits, kept with the heart (up to 60).
  • Pictures of Rory, taken with the camera button. They're pictures the game draws of Rory, not photos: the device's camera is never used.

Deleting the app deletes all of these.

Pictures

Pictures of Rory stay in the game's album. A grown-up can copy them out with the Files app (On My iPhone › Rory Bears › Pictures). There's no Share button in the game, so a child can't send them anywhere from inside it.

No permissions asked

Rory Bears doesn't ask to use the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Children

Rory Bears is made for young children. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.
