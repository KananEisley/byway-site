Dressing Rory

The pictures along the bottom (beside Rory on a wide iPad) are drawers: hats, glasses, things for the neck, clothes, capes and wings, shoes, and things to hold. Tap a drawer, then tap a thing to put it on, and tap it again to take it off. Each drawer scrolls sideways when it holds more than fits. Rory wears one thing in each place at a time.

The row of coloured dots changes the colour of what's in that drawer.

The animals drawer chooses whether Rory is a bear, a tiger, a bunny, a dog, a cat, a donkey, a rhino or a gorilla. The dots below it choose the coat's colour, the same ten for every animal, and the last dot adds or removes spots. Every outfit fits every animal. The place drawer chooses where Rory stands.

The buttons over Rory

  • Start fresh (the round arrow, top left): takes everything off, keeping Rory's animal, colour, spots and place. Tap it again, before changing anything, to put it all back.
  • Sparkles: a surprise outfit.
  • Heart: keeps the outfit as a favourite. It fills in when the outfit is already kept.

Favourite outfits

Kept outfits are in the first drawer, the one with hearts in it. Tap one to dress Rory in it again. To remove some, tap the bin at the end of the row, tap the ones to remove, then tap the tick.

Is it safe for my child?

Rory Bears has no ads, no links out, no Share button and no purchases inside the game, and it never connects to the internet. There's no score, timer or reward for coming back, so it's easy to put down. See the privacy policy.
