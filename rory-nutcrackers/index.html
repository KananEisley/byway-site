Rory Nutcrackers

A pleasant dress-up game for small children.

Paint a wooden nutcracker's face, give it hair and a beard, dress it in soldier's hats, Sugar Plum dresses, Santa suits and more, choose where it stands, and keep the outfits you love. Made for children about 3 to 7, with nothing to read and nothing to win.

iPhone and iPad · Coming soon to the App Store

Lots to try on

208 things to wear and hold

Soldier's hats and jackets, the Mouse King's crown, the Sugar Plum Fairy's tiara and dress, ballet slippers, a drum, a trumpet and a golden walnut. Be a doctor, Santa, a pirate, an elf, a firefighter, a bagpiper or a unicorn, or dress up for space, the Wild West, sports and every holiday.

Seven colours each

Every hat, coat and shoe comes in seven soft colours.

Nutcrackers who look like everyone

Ten face paints from ivory to deep brown, fifteen hairstyles, seven beards and moustaches, and ten colours for the hair and the beard, each chosen on its own.

25 places

A Christmas parlour by the tree, a mantelpiece, the Land of Sweets, a snowy forest, a stage, a castle, the moon and more.

Easy for small hands

No words to read

Everything is a picture, with big buttons. Tap a thing to put it on, and tap again to take it off.

Favourites to come back to

The heart keeps an outfit in a drawer of favourites. Start fresh any time, and put it all back with one tap.

A surprise

The sparkles make a surprise outfit.

Gentle Christmas music

Jingle Bells, Silent Night, Deck the Halls and more, played softly on a music box. A button turns it off, and so does the silent switch.

Pleasant, and safe

  • No ads
  • No score or timer
  • No winning or losing
  • No rewards for coming back
  • Nothing collected

Rory Nutcrackers has no code that connects to the internet, and nothing a child does in it ever leaves the device. Nothing moves on its own, and there's nothing to buy by accident.
