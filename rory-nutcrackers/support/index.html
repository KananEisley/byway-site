Dressing the nutcracker

The pictures along the bottom (beside the nutcracker on a wide iPad) are drawers. Tap a drawer, then tap a thing to put it on, and tap it again to take it off. Each drawer scrolls sideways when it holds more than fits. The nutcracker wears one thing in each place at a time: on its head, over its eyes, on its face, round its neck, its clothes, on its back, its shoes, and something to hold.

The row of coloured dots changes the colour of what's in that drawer.

The first drawers choose the nutcracker itself: its face paint, its hair (the dots below choose the hair's colour) and its beard (the dots below choose the beard's colour, on its own). The place drawer, at the end, chooses where it stands.

The buttons over the nutcracker

  • Start fresh (the round arrow, top left): takes everything off, keeping the face paint, hair, beard and place. Tap it again, before changing anything, to put it all back.
  • Speaker: turns the Christmas music and the little sounds on or off. The iPhone's silent switch turns them off too.
  • Sparkles: a surprise outfit.
  • Heart: keeps the outfit as a favourite. It fills in when the outfit is already kept.

Favourite outfits

Kept outfits are in the first drawer, the one with hearts in it. Tap one to dress the nutcracker in it again. To remove some, tap the bin at the end of the row, tap the ones to remove, then tap the tick.

Is it safe for my child?

Rory Nutcrackers has no ads, no links out, no Share button and no purchases inside the game, and it never connects to the internet. There's no score, timer or reward for coming back, so it's easy to put down. See the privacy policy.
