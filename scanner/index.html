Scanner

Searchable scans you keep.

Turn letters, receipts and forms into PDFs you can search, kept as ordinary files in folders, in your iCloud Drive or on your iPhone. The text is read on your iPhone, and nothing is ever uploaded.

$2.99 · iPhone · No subscription · Coming soon to the App Store

Scan in seconds

Point at a page

The edges are found and the page is straightened and cleaned up, using Apple's own document camera.

As many pages as you like

Scan a whole letter into one PDF. Retake or crop any page before it's saved.

Named your way

Give each scan a name, like "Car insurance", or keep its date. Names always start with the day, so scans stay in order.

Choose the quality

Small, Standard or Best pages, in colour or grayscale.

Search everything

Every page's text is read on your iPhone, so you can find a scan by what it says, not just its name. The text stays inside each PDF too: search it in Finder, Spotlight and any PDF reader, or copy it out.

Yours, as plain files

Ordinary PDFs

Each scan is a PDF that opens anywhere, named for the day it was scanned.

Folders are folders

Make folders in the app, or on your Mac in iCloud Drive › Scanner. They're the same folders.

iCloud Drive or your iPhone

Keep your scans in iCloud Drive, right there in Finder on your Mac, or only on your iPhone.

Straight to a flash drive

Export everything, a folder, or the scans you pick to any folder in Files, including a USB-C or Lightning flash drive.

Private by design

Scanner has no code that connects to the internet, so we never see your scans. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Pay once; there's no subscription.
