Scanner: Privacy

Scanner collects nothing. Your scans are yours, kept as ordinary PDFs in your own iCloud Drive or on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Scanner has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it has no code that connects to the internet. We never see your scans or anything about you or how you use the app.

Where your scans go

Scans are saved as PDF files in your iCloud Drive, in the Scanner folder. Apple stores and syncs that folder for you, under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. If iCloud Drive is off, scans stay on your iPhone until it's on. In Settings you can choose to keep them only on your iPhone instead.

When you export scans, Scanner copies them only to the folder or drive you pick.

Reading the text

The words on each page are read on your iPhone, using Apple's own text recognition, and saved inside the PDF so it can be searched. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this. To make searching quick, Scanner remembers the text of your scans while it's open, and forgets it when it closes.

The camera

Used only to scan your pages. Scans hold the pages themselves and nothing else: no location or other details are added.

Kept on your iPhone

Your choices in Settings (the quality of new scans, colour or grayscale, and where scans are kept) are saved in a small file on your iPhone and never leave it.

Sharing

A scan leaves Scanner only when you send it somewhere: with the Share button, by copying its text, or by exporting it to a folder or drive you pick. It then goes where you send it, through Apple's own tools.

Children

Scanner collects nothing from anyone, children included.
