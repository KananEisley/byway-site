Scanner: Help

Searchable scans you keep, as plain files.

Scanning

Tap Scan and point your iPhone at a page. The edges are found and the page is straightened for you. Keep going for more pages, retake or crop any of them, then tap Save. Scanner then offers to name it; keep the date and time instead if you like.

Where are my scans?

In iCloud Drive, in the Scanner folder: in the Files app on your iPhone, and in Finder on your Mac. Folders are the folders inside it. If iCloud Drive is off, scans wait on your iPhone (Files › On My iPhone › Scanner) and move to iCloud Drive once it's on.

To keep them only on your iPhone instead, choose This iPhone in Settings; Scanner moves them for you. They're then not in iCloud, so copy them somewhere safe now and then with Export.

Searching

Type in the search box at the top of your scans (pull down a little if it's hidden). Scanner looks through names and the words inside every page; every word you type must match, and capitals and accents don't matter. The first search may take a moment while Scanner reads your scans.

Names

Each scan is named for when it was made, like 2026-11-14 09.30.12.pdf. A name you give keeps the day first, like 2026-11-14 Car insurance.pdf, so scans stay in date order. To change it, open the scan, tap the ⋯ button and choose Rename.

Folders

New scans go into the folder you're looking at. Tap New Folder in the row of folders at the top, or make one in the Scanner folder on your Mac. Touch and hold a folder to rename or delete it; deleting can keep its scans (they move to All Scans) or delete them too. To move a scan, open it, tap ⋯ and choose Move to Folder, or use Select to move many at once.

Copying scans to a flash drive or computer

Tap the export button at the top left (the box with an arrow) and pick a folder: on your iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or on a USB-C or Lightning flash drive plugged into your iPhone. Everything shown is copied, with folders as folders; use Select to pick scans. Exporting again skips what's already there.

Quality

In Settings: Small, Standard (the default) or Best, in colour or grayscale. Higher quality is sharper and takes more space. Scans you already have stay as they are.

A new iPhone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, install Scanner, and your scans and folders are there. Deleting the app doesn't delete your scans from iCloud Drive. Scans kept only on your iPhone are not in iCloud: export them before you change phones.
